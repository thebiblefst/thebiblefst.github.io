Why this exists

A Christian faith willing to ask difficult questions.

I'm exploring the Bible systematically, from Genesis to Revelation, trying to understand what its authors meant, what the evidence supports and which Christian interpretations stand up to careful scrutiny.

I believe God can act miraculously. I also believe established scientific and historical findings matter. This journey is about following the strongest arguments—not defending an answer chosen in advance.

Three lenses, one investigation

Taking faith seriously means taking questions seriously.

Each study begins with the biblical text before assessing outside evidence and rival explanations.

01 /

Scripture first

Read the passage in its original literary, historical and linguistic setting. Ask what it actually claims.

02 /

Evidence matters

Explore science, archaeology and history carefully. Separate what is established from what is debated.

03 /

Conclusions, not slogans

Compare serious interpretations, acknowledge unresolved tensions and explain which reading is strongest.
