About the publication

A journey of Christian faith and honest inquiry.

This is an independent personal exploration of Scripture—not a church, a denomination or an attempt to prove an answer at any cost.

The reason for starting

What should I believe—and why?

I'm working through the Bible systematically, from Genesis to Revelation, because I want to understand Christianity as faithfully and accurately as possible.

I take Scripture seriously and believe that God can act supernaturally. I also believe questions about archaeology, cosmology, evolution, historical evidence and apparently conflicting biblical passages deserve more than a quick answer.

Some investigations may reinforce what I previously believed. Others may challenge a familiar interpretation. And some may end with a question genuinely unresolved.

Rather than hiding that process, I want to share it: the biblical text, the competing explanations, the strongest evidence and the reasoning behind each provisional conclusion.

Independent and non-denominational

The Bible — Faith, Science & Truth is a personal Christian publication. It is not operated by, affiliated with or speaking for any denomination, church or other religious organisation.

What you'll find here

As the investigations are approved for publication, this site will offer accessible long-form articles, supporting references, companion podcast episodes, educational video and links to short-form material on our existing social channels.

All central educational material will be freely accessible. There is no requirement to register, submit personal details or join a mailing list to read the website.

What engagement means here

I want people to be curious, read closely, question conclusions and examine the evidence for themselves. Engagement should come from worthwhile ideas and clear storytelling—not manufactured controversy or misleading claims.

The ambition is simple: reading Scripture carefully, following the evidence and seeking the truth.
