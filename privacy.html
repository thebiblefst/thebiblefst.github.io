Privacy & transparency

Website privacy information

Last reviewed: 13 November 2026

What this website does not ask you to provide

The Bible — Faith, Science & Truth does not run an email subscription form, visitor account system, comment form, payment form or contact database on this website. No analytics, advertising trackers or optional tracking scripts have been added to this site's code.

The site does not intentionally set cookies through its own HTML or CSS. Pages, images and fonts are served locally from this site rather than embedded from advertising, analytics, social-media or third-party font services.

Hosting and technical processing

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub. When a visitor opens a page, necessary technical requests are made to deliver the website. GitHub says that it logs the visitor's IP address for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in. That processing is governed by GitHub's own policies.

See GitHub's explanation of GitHub Pages and GitHub's privacy statement.

Links to external platforms

Links on this website may take you to Spotify, YouTube, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, Pinterest or X. Those services have their own privacy practices and may use cookies or collect information independently when you visit them. No Spotify player, social feeds, video players or tracking pixels from those services are embedded on this website.

Advertising and future changes

The website does not currently display advertising or operate paid subscriptions. If the site's functionality or data practices change, this page will be updated before the relevant feature is introduced.

A note on limits

Minimal data collection by this website does not mean that the hosting provider or a platform you choose to visit collects no technical information. Privacy controls and any legal obligations should be reviewed whenever the website changes.

Corrections and editorial transparency

Substantial published studies will distinguish evidence from interpretation, identify confidence levels and disclose meaningful corrections. Until an investigation is approved for public release, its conclusions remain outside this publication.
