The Bible, Investigated

A journey through the whole Bible.

From Genesis to Revelation, each public study will ask what the passage means, what the evidence supports and what conclusions are most defensible.

The first chapter

Beginning with creation.

The detailed study archive will grow in biblical order. No investigation appears as published before approval.

Publication in preparation

Genesis 1:1–2:3: Six days of creation?

What did the ancient text intend? Why does its arrangement matter? How should its claims be evaluated alongside what science establishes about cosmic and Earth history?

We are preparing an accessible public investigation. Its evidence, competing interpretations and provisional conclusion will be added after editorial approval.

Why isn't there a full article here yet?
